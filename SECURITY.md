# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability privately

Do not report suspected security vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions, pull requests, or other public channels.

Submit a private report through [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/jessepollak/home/security/advisories/new). A GitHub account and sign-in are required to open the private report form.

## What to include

Provide enough sanitized detail to reproduce and assess the issue:

- the affected Home surface, component, route, or workflow, including the commit or version tested when known;
- clear reproduction steps, prerequisites, and the expected and observed behavior;
- the potential impact, affected users or assets, and any conditions required for exploitation; and
- safe evidence such as redacted logs, screenshots, request or response metadata, or a minimal reproduction using synthetic data.

If evidence cannot be shared safely, submit a redacted description first rather than attaching sensitive material.

## Protect secrets, customer data, and funds

Do not include live secrets, tokens, customer data, wallet credentials, private keys, seed or recovery material, one-time codes, or any other funded transaction authority in a report. Do not move funds or submit a funded transaction to demonstrate an issue. Revoke or rotate exposed credentials through the relevant provider instead of sending them to this repository.

This policy does not establish acknowledgement or response-time commitments.
