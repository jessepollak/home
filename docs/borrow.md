# Borrow

Jesse-locked September 13, 2026 ([#395](https://github.com/jessepollak/home/issues/395)); five-market admission for [#649](https://github.com/jessepollak/home/issues/649).

## Launch boundary

Borrow projects only operator-approved entries from the compile-time [verified Morpho registry](morpho-markets.md) on Base. Five USDC-loan markets are enabled: cbBTC, cbXRP, cbETH, cbDOGE, and cbADA. Each has its own isolated Morpho tuple, LLTV, oracle, and collateral decimals. Protocol listings and Morpho API discovery never grant transaction authority. The reader, bigint math, calldata builders, and action preparation derive market-specific values from the registry entry rather than a default pair.

An operator warning or loss of admission usefulness changes a retained registry entry to `reducing-only`, **never deletes it**. This retains existing position access: repay (including full repayment), add collateral, and zero-debt collateral withdrawal remain available when required reads verify. Borrow-more, atomic supply-and-borrow, and debt-bearing collateral withdrawal are blocked. Repay-all and atomic close remain backend operations, not separate visible actions.

Every read recomputes `keccak256(abi.encode(loanToken, collateralToken, oracle, irm, lltv))`, verifies the onchain `idToMarketParams` tuple and the loan/collateral `decimals()` at the pinned block, and fails closed for that market on mismatch. Market snapshots must have a nonzero oracle price. A single bounded read pins one Base block for all markets, chunks initial `eth_call`s and IRM rate calls into batches of at most 40 requests, then reconfirms its hash once. An individual invalid market is unavailable rather than a fabricated zero; a chain, transport, or reorg failure invalidates the entire batch. Preparing actions independently verifies its snapshot, simulates the exact ordered Coinbase smart-account batch, and reconfirms the pinned block. The server derives the owner, `onBehalf`, receiver, Morpho deployment, tokens, oracle, IRM, and LLTV; the client supplies only a configured id, operation, and decimal-integer base-unit amounts.

## Borrow interface

Signed-in users open `/borrow` to see Borrowed, Open loans and Assets you can borrow against. The summary uses Home's priced debt value only with complete valuation and complete market discovery whose per-market debts agree within one loan-token cent; otherwise it displays exact USDC units. It never treats an unavailable read as zero. Open-loan rows show exact debt units, market APR and risk tiers; zero-debt pledged collateral stays in Open loans as “Collateral available” / “No debt.” Eligible assets held in the wallet show borrowing capacity, while unavailable or unheld assets are inert. Partial discovery displays Retry and a qualified total.

Selecting a loan row opens the collateral-named management sheet. Its footer holds Repay, Borrow more, Add collateral and Withdraw with the established eligibility gates; health factor, limits and last check sit behind Details. A held-asset row opens a deliberate Borrow entry sheet. Money actions follow a sequential sheet-to-dialog handoff and return to the current market sheet with focus on the action where possible, or to Borrowed if the position or market disappears. The `market` query parameter still opens the direct Borrow MoneyModal for configured ids; it does not enter the overview sheet. [Adopted Direction A](borrow-overview-design.md) records the hierarchy, derivations and follow-ups.

A user without a position enters the USDC amount first. Available borrowing is derived from verified wallet collateral, market liquidity, Morpho share rounding, and the `1.25` hard health-factor floor. Home targets `1.50` when opening; if the requested amount cannot reach that target with the wallet balance but still passes `1.25`, the available collateral is used. The review states the exact collateral amount to be locked. Risk copy uses the collateral's display name: “<displayName> can fall X% before liquidation.” The price-drop buffer is `(health factor - WAD) * 10000 / health factor` in bps. The meter clamps at 50%, with a 20% floor and approximately 33% healthy ticks; numeric health factor is secondary. Oracle raw price uses `10^(36 + loanDecimals - collateralDecimals)` units per USD, not a universal 36-decimal scale.

Borrow review includes the amount, movements, projected liquidation buffer, variable rate from the prepared snapshot, Base network, and compact server warnings. Prepared reviews expire after two minutes. Repay below estimated debt is exact partial repayment. Max or an amount at or above estimated debt uses share-based repay-all with a finite wallet-bounded maximum; if wallet USDC is below debt, Max remains a partial repayment. The client buffer is approximately one hour of rate-based accrual plus one base unit; the server determines exact shares and the reviewed maximum. Confirmation remains a thin commit of stored calls.

## Private APIs

- `GET /api/borrow` returns overview **version 2**: configured opportunities, verified nonzero positions, owner/provider scope, complete/partial status, one `discovery.sourceBlock`, and a full projected detail `snapshot` for each available opportunity. All available snapshots and positions share the same block number and hash. `sourceBlock` is null only if no market is available. Failed markets are unavailable; their snapshots are omitted.
- `GET /api/borrow/markets/:marketId` returns detail version 1: verified identity, source, market/wallet state, accrued position, raw limits, and policy-adjusted limits.
- `POST /api/actions/prepare` supports add collateral, borrow, atomic supply-and-borrow, partial repay, capped share-based repay-all, collateral withdrawal, and atomic close. Borrow metadata includes fresh `borrowAprWad`.

Risk-increasing actions that leave debt require projected health factor `>= 1.25`; zero-debt closes and withdrawals are exempt. Approvals are finite and exact, with no `approve(0)` precursor or unlimited approval. No live-provider, funded-wallet, or real-money test is claimed.

## Admission usefulness and limits

At admission and again at implementation, a $10,000 USDC borrow must raise the current annualized variable rate by **less than 1 percentage point** while leaving **at least $100,000 USDC** of available liquidity. At finalized Base block **51714405** (`0x2de4e4f50c66b50715e05fd60f43edbcb2b735ab28bae2840a2806c19ffb78bd`, 2026-09-24T02:49:17Z), cbXRP left $5,656,001.40 and changed 6.1939% → 6.2252% (+0.031pp); cbETH left $1,324,396.26 and changed 4.9747% → 5.0792% (+0.104pp); cbDOGE left $253,554.97 and changed 6.1802% → 6.8661% (+0.686pp); cbADA left $222,471.72 and changed 6.1994% → 6.9600% (+0.761pp). cbBTC had $160.97m available before the same test borrow. Rates use IRM `borrowRateView` at block timestamp, with totalBorrowAssets increased by $10,000 USDC and annualization at 31,536,000 seconds. This is an admission-time floor, not an assertion that future rates or liquidity remain unchanged.

The bounded overview verifies only registry candidates. Discovering positions outside the retained registry needs a separately trusted source; third-party discovery must not authorize transactions. There are no background liquidation alerts, live-provider tests, or funded-wallet CI tests.
