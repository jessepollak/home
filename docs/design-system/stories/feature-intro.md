# FeatureIntro

Option C is the adopted feature-intro standard (#895). The owned component and its states are shown in `UI/FeatureIntro`; Card states are not mounted in the product until #821.

- Card: `ui-feature-intro--card-not-issued`, `ui-feature-intro--card-not-issued-desktop`, `ui-feature-intro--card-pending`, `ui-feature-intro--card-loading`, `ui-feature-intro--card-needs-verification`, `ui-feature-intro--card-region-unavailable`
- Content and direction: `ui-feature-intro--long-copy`, `ui-feature-intro--no-illustration`, `ui-feature-intro--rtl`
- Sheet: `ui-feature-intro--sheet`, `ui-feature-intro--sheet-pending`, `ui-feature-intro--sheet-unavailable`
- Save: `ui-feature-intro--save-not-started`, `ui-feature-intro--save-not-started-desktop`
