# Operator fees

- Fee settings: `operator-fee-settings--default`, `operator-fee-settings--stored-with-destination`, `operator-fee-settings--destination-change-confirmation`, `operator-fee-settings--conflict`, `operator-fee-settings--save-error`. The Settings form edits the swap fee (basis points with the percent equivalent) and the revenue destination; a new destination needs a full-address confirmation before saving.
- Fee revenue: `operator-revenue--empty`, `operator-revenue--mixed-results`. Expected fees, a 30-day daily list with a text alternative per day, and recent fees with result and explorer link.
