# Theme

The shared sheet spring uses the owned motion contract; its stiffness targets an approximately 350 ms critical settle across a full-height sheet, with damping chosen to prevent overshoot. Home mark enter and exit sequences are not interruptible or reentrant by design, matching the reference interaction exactly.

This section describes the implemented system and maintenance defaults. [UI direction](../ui-direction.md#sources-and-status) records exploration and acceptance status; production defaults do not freeze the visual choices of an explicitly scoped experiment.

`apps/web/app/globals.css` uses shadcn's stock neutral theme generated by the `base-nova` preset. Home overrides `--primary` with Base blue (`#0052ff` Light; `#578bfa` Dark with a near-black `--primary-foreground`), keeps `--ring` a neutral ink (`oklch(0.556 0 0)` Light; `oklch(0.708 0 0)` Dark) so focus never reads as a blue highlight, `--primary-foreground` with white in Light, `--radius` with `0.25rem`, and `--destructive` with the reviewed softer red (`#c8372d` Light). Semantic status tokens are `--market-gain` / `--market-loss` for money-in and price-change text and the price-chart line, `--warning` for warning titles, icons and the pending status dot, and `--status-positive` / `--status-caution` / `--status-negative` for coverage traffic dots (`bg-status-*`); each has a `@theme` colour and a separately checked Dark value. Status colour stays on an Alert or Toast's title and icon; body copy and actions stay `foreground`. `--payout-*` brand tokens and their `-foreground` pairs stay in `:root` only: payout brand marks keep their brand colors in both themes. `--balance-bar-pending-cash-out` is a `:root`-only hatched fill derived from `--balance-bar-cash`; the Home allocation bar and legend use it for Pending cash-out. What `globals.css` may contain is pinned by `scripts/gates/globals-css-allowlist.json`.

## Appearance

Home ships Light and Dark appearances from one token set: `:root` is Light and `.dark` on `<html>` is Dark. No component has a separate dark implementation. Both themes keep the same surface order: the page is `--muted`, and headers, cards, sheets and popovers use `--background`/`--card`/`--popover`, so raised surfaces are lighter than the page in both. `--overlay` dims the page behind a sheet. `color-scheme` follows the theme so native inputs, scrollbars and form controls match, and `<meta name="theme-color">` tracks `--background`.

- **Preference.** Account → Preferences → Appearance offers Light, Dark and System (the default). The choice is stored in this browser's `localStorage` (`home.appearance.v1`), not on the account, and survives sign-out. System follows OS changes while Home is open; Light and Dark ignore them. Unreadable storage falls back to System. A failed write keeps the choice for the visit and announces that it was not saved.
- **Owner.** `apps/web/shared/appearance/preference.ts` owns the preference model. `apps/web/client/appearance/` owns the pre-paint head script, the `useAppearance` store and the single `AppearanceSync` root listener mounted by `app/layout.tsx`. Do not toggle `.dark` anywhere else in the app.
- **First paint.** The head script resolves the theme before the body renders, so cold loads, reloads and deep links never show the wrong theme, and nothing remounts. A theme change suppresses CSS transitions for that single switch.
- **New colours.** Add a semantic token with a checked Light and Dark value instead of a literal colour. Text pairs need 4.5:1; graphics, focus rings and status dots need 3:1 against the card and the page in both themes. Canvas and WebGL colours read 6-digit hex tokens from computed style and re-read them when `.dark` changes (see the landing globe). `useAppearance().resolvedAppearance` reports the theme applied to `<html>`, not a separate calculation, so the price chart passes it to its chart library and matches every other surface, including the Storybook toolbar. Brand marks (`--payout-*` and the Home mark) keep their brand colours.
- **Review.** Storybook has a `Theme` toolbar global (Light/Dark). It toggles `.dark` on the preview document root, so portaled popups, drawers and toasts follow it. Every story resets to Light unless it sets `globals: { theme: "dark" }`. Representative `UI/*` stories export a `Dark` story, and the `Journeys/Account Appearance` stories walk the Account control in both themes. `apps/web/tests/browser/appearance.pw.ts` covers resolution, persistence, OS changes and first paint.

Use the stock system sans and monospace stacks: there is no `next/font` setup or font asset directory. Money and other aligned numbers use `tabular-nums`; monospace is reserved for addresses, hashes, and code. Use the stock Tailwind type scale and component spacing.

## Elevation

Four levels, each a `--elevation-*` value in `:root` and `.dark` exposed as a `shadow-*` utility. Each level carries its own 1px foreground ring, so a surface with a level draws no separate `border` or `ring-1`; focus rings still compose on top.

| Level | Utility | Surfaces | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised | `shadow-raised` | Card, pull-to-refresh indicator, Switch thumb, conversation scroll button | ring foreground/10 + `0 1px 2px` black/5% | ring foreground/10, no shadow |
| Floating | `shadow-floating` | Popover, Select and Combobox lists, coverage status preview | ring foreground/10 + `shadow-md` | ring foreground/15 + `0 4px 12px` black/40% |
| Overlay | `shadow-overlay` | Dialog, Drawer | ring foreground/10 + `shadow-lg` | ring foreground/10, no shadow (the scrim separates it) |
| Toast | `shadow-toast` | Toast | ring foreground/10 + `shadow-lg` | ring foreground/15 + `0 8px 24px` black/50% |

Dark drops or deepens shadows because a black shadow on the darker page reads as a smudge; the ring carries the edge.

## Interaction states

Neutral controls (Button `outline`, `ghost`, `navigation` and `floating-control`, Item rows that are links or buttons, Toggle and Toggle Group, Select and Combobox items) draw every state from one contract. Filled variants (`default`, `secondary`, `destructive`) keep their own tone steps.

| State | Treatment | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hover / highlight | `bg-state-hover` with foreground text; a menu's keyboard highlight matches pointer hover | foreground 5% | foreground 7% |
| Pressed | `active:bg-state-pressed` while held | foreground 8% | foreground 11% |
| Focus-visible | `border-ring` + 3px `ring-ring/50`, inset on flush rows | unchanged | unchanged |
| Disabled | `opacity-50` over the rest fill; no extra fill, no hover | | |
| Selected | `bg-state-pressed` with foreground text (`aria-pressed`, `aria-expanded`, `data-pressed`) | foreground 8% | foreground 11% |

Fields (Input, Textarea, Input Group, Select and Combobox triggers, native select) darken their border on hover (`hover:border-input-hover`, foreground 24% over `--input`) instead of tinting; focus and invalid borders win over hover. `Button press` keeps its scale feedback alongside the pressed tint.

## Radius

`--radius` stays `0.25rem`; every step is a multiple of it.

| Surface | Step |
| --- | --- |
| Controls: Button, Input, Textarea, Select and Combobox triggers and items, Toggle, Input OTP, Input Group, Skeleton | `rounded-lg` |
| Row / card: Card, standalone Item, Alert, Empty, support messages | `rounded-xl` |
| Popup: Popover, Select and Combobox lists, coverage status preview, Toast | `rounded-xl` |
| Sheet: Drawer, Dialog, the desktop money sheet | `rounded-2xl` |
| Pill / avatar: Badge, Switch, Progress, rail and tab buttons, search field, avatars, status marks | `rounded-full` |
| Marks nested in a control: Input Group buttons, Kbd, Item images, segmented choices, inline and balance buttons | `rounded-sm` |

Owned components do not use `rounded-xs`, `rounded-md` or `rounded-4xl`. Prompt Input keeps its `rounded-3xl` until it is assigned a role.

## Type roles

Roles use the stock scale and three weights (normal, medium, semibold).

| Role | Classes | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amount | `text-4xl font-semibold leading-none tabular-nums` | Balances and confirm summaries; the amount field keeps `text-5xl` as its fit ceiling |
| Heading | `text-base font-semibold` | One per surface: Card, Dialog, Drawer, Popover, Result and Feature intro titles |
| Body | `text-sm` | Descriptions and message text; fields render `text-base` below `md` so iOS does not zoom on focus |
| Label | `text-sm font-medium` | Buttons, toggles, row titles, field labels |
| Metadata | `text-xs text-muted-foreground` | Captions and compact descriptions; badges keep `font-medium` |
| Numeric | `tabular-nums` | A modifier for any aligned or changing number |
| Mono | `font-mono text-sm` | Addresses, hashes and code only |

Country selection and searchable asset selection use the `Combobox`; its value truncates by default. Simple non-searchable pickers use Base UI `Select`. Financial rows stay on `Item`; do not introduce Data Table on mobile.

Use the owned component contracts rather than restyling their slots:
- `ItemMedia variant="avatar"` owns the standard circular row media. `ItemTitle` accepts `tone` (`default | muted | primary | gain | destructive`), `numeric`, and `truncate` (`true` clamps to one line, `false` keeps one unwrapped line, `wrap` wraps anywhere, `stacked` allows two lines when a finance row stacks); `ItemDescription` accepts `lines={1 | 2 | "wrap"}` (one line truncates with an ellipsis; `wrap` shows the full description without a line clamp) and `size="xs"` for a compact 12 px description. Finance-row context and value context share the default description size.
- Finance rows (`AssetRow`, `BalanceRow`, `ActivityRow`) keep label and value on one line at normal text size. When a row is narrower than 14rem (for example at 200% text on a phone), the value stacks under the label at the inline end and wraps rather than clipping.
- `CardContent inset="list"` owns list-card horizontal insets; put screen-specific flow spacing on a plain inner wrapper.
- Grouped money rows (review summary, transaction receipt) use `Card variant="flush"` with `CardContent inset="list"`; rows inside carry `px-3` and no dividers.
- A hosted detail page (asset detail in Invest and Investments) uses `Card variant="page"`: a flat surface that bleeds to the screen edges below `sm` and a bordered card on the muted page from `sm` up. It clips only horizontally, so sticky children such as the trade bar keep sticking.
- `Button variant="navigation"` owns primary-navigation presentation and has no transition, so selection, hover and focus-ring changes are instant and never leave the compositor; `size="tab"` owns the 54px icon-over-label capsule tab used below 1024px, and the desktop rail uses `size="lg"` rows. The capsule tabs sit on liquid glass. It has a lazy lens pill in every engine and rim refraction in Chromium, and it falls back to an opaque capsule under reduced transparency, forced colours, or no `backdrop-filter`. See [Liquid material](../design-explorations/glass-navigation.md#liquid-material). `size="inline"` is for small actions embedded in prose.
- `Button variant="balance-segment"` and `variant="balance-legend"` (with the matching sizes) own the Total balance allocation selection: segments keep their proportional width and gain only a vertical hit area, dim when another category is selected and grow in height when selected; legend buttons carry keyboard focus and `aria-pressed`, and switch to semibold foreground text with no background when selected. Both use `data-selected="true"`, and reduced motion drops the transition.
- `Button press="standard" | "icon" | "none"` owns press feedback: standard actions compress on pointer/touch-down, icon-only controls compress deliberately more, and `none` keeps wide rows, navigation, and link-like text still as whole surfaces. Every variant mirrors its hover treatment in `active:` and reduced motion drops every press transform while keeping those color cues.
- `Button size="touch"` is the 44px-minimum touch target for primary mobile actions; it wraps long labels instead of clipping. Use it instead of `h-11` overrides.
- `InputOTP` / `InputOTPGroup` / `InputOTPSlot` own one-box-per-digit verification with a single accessible input. `Input variant="code"` owns monospace input typography; `InputGroupInput` forwards the same variant.
- `PayoutMark variant="cashapp" | "zelle" | "monzo" | "revolut" | "fallback" | "count"` owns circular payout-mark geometry, typography, and semantic brand colors.
- `Button loading` owns the submitting state: it keeps the label and focus, adds `aria-busy` and a leading spinner, and ignores presses. `MoneyConfirmFooter submitting` applies it to the confirm primary.
- `ResultHeader outcome="success" | "pending" | "failed" | "unknown"` owns the money result header, and `StatusSteps` / `StatusStep status="complete" | "current" | "upcoming" | "failed"` own real transfer stages. Money flows compose them through `MoneyResult` / `MoneyResultFooter`; `unknown` never offers a retry. Use StatusStep, not Progress, for transfers.
- `Switch` is the semantic on/off control. Drawer surface, title, header, footer, safe-area, shadow, and immediate-motion treatment are owned defaults.
- `RadioGroup` is the mutually exclusive choice control for funding/payout choices confirmed by a separate action; direct-action rows stay `Item`/`Button`. `RadioGroup variant="segmented"` with `RadioGroupSegment` is the compact single-choice segmented control (Appearance); each segment keeps a 44px minimum touch height.
- `CopyableValue presentation="reveal"` shows a one-line condensed address; its `Popover` exposes the complete selectable address and a Copy button on review and Account surfaces.
- `Popover` owns small anchored disclosures, such as the Home header status; its content needs an accessible name (`aria-label`). `Skeleton` owns the loading tone (foreground at 10%), which stays visible on both cards and the muted page.
