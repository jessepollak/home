### `card`
- **Entry context**: card · `/card` · same · session + `/api/cards` fixture, server started with `BRIDGE_CARDS_ENABLED=1` · Main navigation `Card` button between Home and Invest (primary-navigation.tsx), goto path.

- **Live**: read-only for card controls; routine spending allowance verification stops at review; a live confirm follows the operating manual's Rung 3 table.
- **Owned paths**: `apps/web/client/cards/**`, `apps/web/app/api/cards/**`, `apps/web/server/cards/**`, `apps/web/shared/cards/**`
- **Confirm labels**: `Set limit`, `Turn off` (card-allowance MoneyConfirmFooter carries `data-money-action-id`; stop before pressing either during routine verification). Card issuance and lock writes remain unmarked.
- **Reach**: Start the fixture server with `BRIDGE_CARDS_ENABLED=1` (and a synthetic `NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=pk_test_…` only to show `Card details`), seed the session, then `open "/card"`. The shared fixture answers `**/api/cards` with an active card ending 4821 (`cardsBody()` in `tests/browser/fixtures/bodies.ts`). For another state, `network unroute "**/api/cards"` then `network route "**/api/cards" --body '<cardsBody(state) JSON>'` and reopen `/card`. For the lock flow, also route `**/api/cards/ic_fixture4821/freeze` (or `/unfreeze`) with `{"version":1,"card":{"id":"ic_fixture4821","status":"frozen"}}`, re-route `**/api/cards` to the frozen state, then click the `Lock card` switch.
- **Verify**: manual
- **Spending Reach**: With an issued card, the shared `**/api/cards/spending` fixture shows a $25.00 limit. `Change` → enter a positive USDC amount → `Continue` → review; `Turn off` or retired-program `Remove` prepares a revoke review. Inject a `card-allowance` prepare response before walking reviews: the shared generic prepare fixture remains Send-only. Stop before marked confirm controls.
- **Spending Expect**: One `Spending` section after all issued-card overviews. Loading is busy; unavailable offers `Try again` with no figures; not-configured says `Not available yet` with disabled `Set limit`. Ready shows `Available to spend`, limit (`Not set` for zero, `Unlimited` for large allowances), `Set limit`/`Change` disabled until `setEnabled` and active/frozen card state allow setting. Current and nonzero retired permissions remain removable when setting is disabled. Review shows the full prepared spender, `Card purchases from Cash`, current/new limits, Base and expiry. Rejected wallet requests return to review; owner changes close the sheet and fence late responses.
- **Spending without a live card**: Every otherwise-rendered card state shows a revoke-only `Spending` section only for ready data with a nonzero current or retired allowance: `Turn off` for the current spender and `Remove` for each nonzero retired spender, without availability, limit-setting rows or controls. Zero or unknown allowances hide this section. A stale-session execution stays at review with `Your account changed. Sign in again and try again.`, a disabled confirm and enabled Back/close; it is not a submission.
- **Spending sheet lifetime**: While a spending sheet is open, zero/unknown allowances or loading/failed/unavailable card reads hide section rows without closing the review, pending execution or result. Explicit close dismisses the sheet; an owner change closes it and ignores late wallet results and refreshes.
- **Spending opener**: Change, Set limit, Turn off and each retired Remove carry the activating DOM control explicitly through the entry to MoneyModal. Close returns to that connected control, not whichever element was focused before activation. Removed rows are not refocused; existing owner boundaries suppress old-owner restoration.
- **Notes**: Without `BRIDGE_CARDS_ENABLED=1` the Card tab is absent and `/card` redirects to `/home`. The `**/api/cards` fixture glob also answers `POST /api/cards`, so `Create your card` shows its error toast unless that POST is routed to a write response. `Get your card`/`Verify` post `/api/cards/enrollment` and navigate the page to the returned Bridge-hosted link; route that POST before clicking. Real card details need a Stripe test-mode publishable key and card; with a synthetic key the sheet shows `Couldn't show card details.` after Stripe rejects the nonce.
- **Expect**: header title `Card`; Main navigation `Card` current. States: not enrolled → `Spend your Cash with a card` + `Get your card`; verification required → `Getting your card` steps + `Verify`; verification pending → steps with `Checking your details` current and no action; ready to issue → `Create your card`; ineligible → `Card isn't available for your account`; active → card image `Virtual card ending 4821`, `Card details` (only with a publishable key) and `Your card` with the `Lock card` switch; frozen → centered `Locked` badge on the card, switch on, `New purchases are declined`; restricted → `Your card is on hold`, switch disabled when locked (`Can't unlock while on hold`), no `Card details`; restricted with no issued card → `Your card is on hold` + `You can't create a card right now.`, no action; several live cards → one block per card newest first, headed `Card ending NNNN` with `Card details ending NNNN` and `Lock card ending NNNN` (cards sharing a last four add their position: `Card 2 ending NNNN`, `Card details for card 2 ending NNNN`, `Lock card 2 ending NNNN`); canceled → `Your card was canceled` + `Get a new card`; unavailable or a failed read (including a failed re-read after a write) → `Card is unavailable right now` + `Try again`.
- **States**: loading (`Loading card`, busy); lock/unlock success toasts `Card locked`/`Card unlocked` only after a completed re-read shows the card in the new status; failures toast `Couldn't lock your card. Try again.`, `Couldn't unlock your card. Try again.`, `Couldn't start verification. Try again.`, `Couldn't create your card. Try again.`, or `Couldn't refresh your card. Try again.` when the write succeeded but the re-read failed, paused offline, answered unavailable, or still showed the old status; `Card details` sheet loading rows then `Card number`, `Expires`, `CVV` rendered by Stripe Issuing Elements iframes, or `Couldn't show card details.` + `Try again`.
