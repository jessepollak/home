- **Owned paths**: `apps/web/client/account/account-settings.tsx`, `apps/web/client/support/support-provider.tsx`, `apps/web/components/{profile-mark,primary-navigation}.tsx`, `apps/web/client/home/shell-chrome.tsx`, `apps/web/client/account/account-route-provider.tsx`, `apps/web/client/account/cdp-client.tsx`, `apps/web/client/account/use-invite-link.ts`, `apps/web/client/account/use-account-export.ts`, `apps/web/client/account/account-export-file.ts`, `apps/web/client/account/smoke-fixture-provider.tsx`, `apps/web/tests/browser/account-export.pw.ts`, `apps/web/client/account/{account-deletion-view,account-deletion-recovery}.tsx`, `apps/web/client/account/{use-account-deletion,account-deletion-device,account-deletion-receipt-recovery}.ts`, `apps/web/tests/browser/account-leave.pw.ts`, `apps/web/shared/invites/**`, `apps/web/client/home/shell.tsx`, `apps/web/client/home/use-show-small-balances.ts`, `apps/web/client/appearance/**`, `apps/web/shared/appearance/**`

- **Notes**: The fixture-session helper seeds signed-in state and fixtures. The Chromium smoke opens the header profile mark with the keyboard, asserts focus enters the settings region, closes through Done and browser Back, restores the exact opener, exercises deep-link and forward-history entry, and verifies every primary-navigation `aria-controls` target remains unique and present. Live: Peer availability varies by deployment and corridor (`PEER_OFFRAMP_ENABLED`, `apps/web/server/funding/providers/peer/manifest.ts`). - **Opener contract**: Header and rail account controls already pass their activating DOM button explicitly to the shell. A deep-link/history entry has no activation opener; closing intentionally falls back to a visible rail/header control, then the panel when neither exists. [Routing Playwright](../../../../apps/web/tests/browser/routing.pw.ts) covers exact-opener return, deep-link and Forward entry.
- **Expect**: region `aria-label="Account settings"` receives programmatic focus without selecting an input; `Show small balances` switch (`getByRole("switch", { name: "Show small balances" })`, mobile-geometry.pw.ts; owned by client/home/use-show-small-balances.ts + account-settings region of shell.tsx). `Appearance` radiogroup (`getByRole("radiogroup", { name: "Appearance" })`) has Light, Dark, and System radios, with one checked; selection updates immediately via shell.tsx. Sign-out control and region selector live here (shell.tsx passes `regionId`, `resolutionSource`, `onRegionChange`, `onSignOut` to client/account/account-settings.tsx). `Invite friends` appears between Account and Disclosures & terms for an available signed-in invite link; the full link is selectable and Copy copies its absolute URL. Share appears only when the Web Share API is supported. `Support` row opens the Support sheet, with `Message us` and an unread count badge when replies arrive; the header profile mark has an unread dot and accessible count.
- **Export**: For a signed-in owner, Your data appears immediately before Disclosures & terms. Export issues a read-only GET to `/api/account/export` and downloads `home-data-export-YYYY-MM-DD.json`; device preferences and private-cache presence metadata are appended without cache contents. The dedicated [export browser regression](../../../../apps/web/tests/browser/account-export.pw.ts) covers file contents, retry, malformed responses, Back, sign-out and owner switch; the shared Reach replay stops at Account entry and does not generate an export.
- **Leave Home**: Your data also offers Leave Home. Its review offers Export my data, states the deletion and five-year pseudonymized retention boundary, and keeps Delete my Home account disabled until the current request status loads; Back changes nothing. Confirm POSTs `/api/account/deletion` (empty body). Queued shows blocker counts and Check status, and a failed attempt is never shown as complete. Completed clears this device's private caches before sign-out and shows the per-store receipt with Download receipt. Fixture sessions have no database, so the status read is unavailable (Couldn't check your deletion request; Try again); route `/api/account/deletion` with a receipt body to see queued or completed. The [leave browser regression](../../../../apps/web/tests/browser/account-leave.pw.ts) and `journeys-account-leave--*` stories cover these states. Live: never confirm deletion on a real or bot account; destructive proof uses synthetic fixtures only.
- **States**: export idle (Export), generating (disabled Preparing… and polite Preparing your export), success (Export downloaded; fresh Export available), failure (Nothing in your account changed; Try again), stale request discarded on Back, sign-out or owner switch; support summary pending (`Checking messages`), successful zero/positive, initial failure with Retry, cached positive failure labelled possibly out of date, cached zero failure stays unknown, no-provider/signed-out has no support status; preference not ready (`isPreferenceReady`); region override messages (`preferenceMessage`); appearance Light / Dark / System (including system changes) and storage-unavailable announcement in the polite preference status; narrow 320px row wraps the radiogroup under its title; invite-link loading shows a labelled skeleton, unavailable or missing account hides the section, and other errors show Try again; support sheet loading, empty, and load-error retry.
- **Regions**: the Country selector lists only the operator's offered countries (all configured countries in fixture sessions, which have no database). A saved country that is no longer offered shows an empty selector with Home in the global presentation.
