- **Notes**: The fixture Reach stops at Buy amount entry, before `Continue` prepares a quote. The smoke asset-detail path also clicks Main navigation `Invest`, clicks the asset row matching `/^NVIDIA/`, expects `/invest/nvdac`, and reads `NVIDIA` from `[data-shell-header-title]`. Live discovery remains read-only; a live trade review may be walked up to its marked `data-money-action-id` control only under the verification ladder and account-anchor checks. A live Buy/Sell confirm is not mapped (bounds tracked in the issue): never press it.
- **Expect**: hub shelves `Stocks`, `Crypto`, `Memes` (`discoverShelves`, client/invest/discover.ts; shelf CardTitle `role=heading aria-level=3`, discover-shelf.tsx); `See all ›` per shelf; empty shelf copy `Loading`/`Unavailable`/`None trending` (discover-shelf.tsx `shelfStatusLabel`); category screen `Back to Invest` (category-screen.tsx); asset detail price (`data-tone`), change (`data-money-change`), `Trade <displayName>` group (client/trading/trade-actions.tsx). With `/api/trades` available and positive USDC/cbBTC balances, Bitcoin has enabled `Buy`/`Sell`, opening `Buy Bitcoin`/`Sell Bitcoin` amount steps. `Continue` prepares the quote; the review title is `Confirm` and rows show `From`, `You pay`, `You receive (estimated)`, `Minimum received`, `Slippage`, `Price`, quoted fee rows if any, `Network` (Base), and `Quote expires in`. Review controls are `Buy $<amount>`/`Sell <amount> BTC`, `Back`, and `Close`.
- **States**: hub loading/empty/error per shelf (`MemeShelfStatus`); category pagination error `Retry`; detail status screen variant (asset-detail-screen.tsx `AssetDetailStatusScreen`). Stocks show `Stocks aren't available yet.` when signed out or without a smart account, `Checking stock trading…` while fetching, `Stock trading isn't available right now.` on fetch failure, `Stock buys aren't available in your location.` when restricted, and `Stocks can't be traded in Home yet.` when eligible but not routed; other crypto have disabled Buy/Sell and `Swaps aren't available right now.`. Unavailable or unreadable trading availability shows `Trading isn't available right now.` or, for an unsupported signer, `Trading isn't available for this account.`. With available trading but zero balances, Buy shows `No Cash available to buy Bitcoin.` and Sell shows `No Bitcoin available to sell.`; the corresponding action is disabled. An expired/stale or already-used quote requires `Get new quote` (never confirm an expired review); a rejected wallet request keeps the review and requires a new quote once it expires; a wallet signing failure before submission keeps the review and allows `Get new quote` after expiry; an unresolved dispatch shows `Retry` to record the same action without a second dispatch until the quote expires, then only `Close` with `This quote expired before the outcome was recorded. Check Activity before trading again.`; an onchain failure directs the user to check Activity before trading again.
