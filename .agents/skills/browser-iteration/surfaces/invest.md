- **Expect**: hub shelves `Stocks`, `Crypto`, `Memes` (`discoverShelves`, client/invest/discover.ts; shelf CardTitle `role=heading aria-level=3`, discover-shelf.tsx); `See all ›` per shelf; empty shelf copy `Loading`/`Unavailable`/`None trending` (discover-shelf.tsx `shelfStatusLabel`); category screen `Back to Invest` (category-screen.tsx); asset detail price (`data-tone`), change (`data-money-change`), `Trade <displayName>` group (client/trading/trade-actions.tsx). An exact-address page `/invest/base:0x…` that is not in the discover catalog shows the token identity (chain symbol when signed in, otherwise the shortened address) on Base, without a chart when there is no price history; it shows `Asset unavailable` when the lookup answers with no result (for example a pool contract). Any non-stock Base token uses per-asset `/api/trades?assetId=` availability: Buy needs a positive USDC balance, Sell needs a positive chain `balanceBaseUnits`, and each opens `Buy <name>`/`Sell <name>` amount steps with a locked header selector showing USDC top-left for Buy or the token for Sell; Sell `Max` prepares `amountBaseUnits: "all"`. `Continue` prepares the quote; the review title is `Confirm`, with the spent amount as headline and summary rows `You get` (estimated receive) and `Network fee` (when paid in USDC). `Details` expands `Minimum received`, `Rate`, `Max slippage`, protocol fee rows if any, `Quote expires in`, `Network` (Base), `From`, and `<symbol> contract` (condensed address; `Show full contract …` reveals the exact traded contract). Review controls are `Buy $<amount>`/`Sell <amount> <symbol>`, `Back`, and `Close`.
- **States**: hub loading/empty/error per shelf (`MemeShelfStatus`); category pagination error `Retry`; detail status screen variant (asset-detail-screen.tsx `AssetDetailStatusScreen`). Stocks show `Stocks aren't available yet.` when signed out or without a smart account, `Checking stock trading…` while fetching, `Stock trading isn't available right now.` on fetch failure, `Stock buys aren't available in your location.` when restricted, and `Stocks can't be traded in Home yet.` when eligible but not routed. Other assets show `Checking trading availability…` while the account resolves or availability is fetching, including while a transient failure (network, 429 or 5xx) is retried; after a settled signed-out session they show `Sign in to trade.`, and after a verified session without a smart account they show `Trading isn't available for this account.`; after a definitive answer or an exhausted retry they show `Trading isn't available right now. Try again later.` for provider or chain unavailability; `Trading isn't available for this account.` for an unsupported signer; `This token couldn't be read on Base. You can still send it.` for an unreadable token; `This asset can't be traded.` for an unsupported asset; and `Buying is unavailable. You can still sell or send.` with Buy disabled and Sell enabled for a buy-blocked asset. A failed check is retried on refocus, reconnect and every 30 seconds. With available trading but zero balances, Buy shows `No Cash available to buy <name>.` and Sell shows `No <symbol> available to sell.`; the corresponding action is disabled. Prepare refusals keep the amount step with a distinct message: route unavailable `No route for this amount. Try a different amount or try again later.`, below minimum `This amount is below the trade minimum. Enter a larger amount.`, unreadable token `This token couldn't be read on Base. Try again later.`, not routed `This asset can't be traded in Home yet.`, and a changed sell balance `Your token balance changed. Review the amount again.`. An expired/stale or already-used quote requires `Get new quote` (never confirm an expired review); a rejected wallet request keeps the review and requires a new quote once it expires; a wallet signing failure before submission keeps the review and allows `Get new quote` after expiry; a lost confirm or handle response shows `Retry` to record the same action without a second dispatch until the quote expires, then only `Close` with `This quote expired before the outcome was recorded. Check Activity before trading again.`; an ambiguous provider dispatch has no Retry and shows Activity guidance. Closing and reopening Buy or Sell permits a new action even while an earlier trade has no recorded outcome; an onchain failure directs the user to check Activity before trading again.
