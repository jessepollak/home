- **Expect**: Overview has Needs attention then Business, each with an unavailable line; sidebar links to Customers, Support, Growth, Money, Settings and Audit log, each with a matching heading. Growth shows Invite links and Attributed sign-ups counts from the registry, or `Invite data isn't available.` when the read fails. Settings shows the regions editor and the Invest pane (Stocks, Crypto and Memes category switches and switches for each configured asset), each with built-in or saved source when persistence is available; without `DATABASE_URL` (fixture and smoke), `Region settings need a database. Home is offering its built-in regions.` and `Invest settings need a database. Home is showing its full catalog.`; when a read fails, `Region settings are unavailable. Try again shortly.` and `Invest settings are unavailable. Try again shortly.` An Invest row with no saved settings uses the built-in catalog without a write; saved rows show a local timestamp and operator address. Hidden discovery categories and assets do not block holdings or direct detail links. Settings also shows the Fees form (Swap fee in bps, Revenue destination, and a confirmation step when the destination changes) or `Fee settings couldn’t load`. Money shows Fee revenue (expected fees, a 30-day list, and recent fees with their result) or `Fee revenue is unavailable. Try again later.` Settings links to Products and markets at `/admin/settings/products`; the pane shows deployment or saved provenance, product modes, vaults and markets, and does not widen code-locked entries. Exit-only parents lock child choices without losing saved values. A pause saves directly; enabling entry shows an inline review requiring `Turn on`. Missing credentials show Invest as Not connected; missing database/corrupt settings show a retry error. Conflict offers reload of current settings. The other sections retain unavailable lines. Current link has `aria-current="page"`; at 390px Sections opens the drawer, navigation closes it and restores menu focus. Signed-out requests redirect to sign-in; non-operator requests redirect to `/home`.
- **States**: loading skeleton; error with `Try again`; Settings review drawer lists offered country changes and their effects; a conflict closes review, preserves the draft and updates the saved baseline; Invest category/asset toggles, a hidden category collapses its asset rows and keeps their individual choices, dirty draft/save pending/saved, 409 conflict adopts the latest value as both baseline and draft so the operator must re-apply their change, failed save allows retry; deployment and saved provenance, exit-only locked children, orphan IDs, 409 conflict/reload, enable review/cancel; `/admin/nope` and nested unknown paths are uncached 404s showing `Page not found` and `Back to Overview`, with no current link.
- **Money in and out** (`/admin/settings/funding`, linked from the Settings index; stories `journeys-admin-funding-settings--*`): deployment-values banner or last saved by/at; provider credential names as set/unset, never values; corridors by region as Connected, off / On / Not connected, with New for corridors added since the last save; `PEER_OFFRAMP_ENABLED` as in effect or set but ignored; notice for saved corridors no longer in the catalog. A not-connected corridor shows its switch on when its saved selection is on, labelled `Not connected · On when credentials are set`, and that switch can be turned off (pausing it) even though turning one on always needs credentials — a disconnected, off corridor's switch stays disabled, and losing a credential clears a pending activation with a notice. Turning any corridor on opens a review (credential status, the evidence note of every provider binding the switch covers, or `none recorded`, and what customers see) and applies only on Confirm; the review also groups corridors recorded on without credentials; pause-only saves confirm inline, naming disconnected corridors being paused and saying such a corridor stays off when its credentials return. States: saved, conflict (Save disabled until the latest settings load), uncertain save result, settings unavailable. Fixture sessions can't reach it; verify in Storybook, then on an operator session.
