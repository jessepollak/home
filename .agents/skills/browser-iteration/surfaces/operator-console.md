### `operator-console`
- **Entry context**: operator · `/admin`, `/admin/{customers,support,growth,money,settings,audit}` · signed native Home operator session · operator allowlist and signed native session (browser admin.pw.ts) · goto `/admin`.
- **Live**: read-only (operator authorization required)
- **Owned paths**: `apps/web/app/admin/**`, `apps/web/client/admin/**`, `apps/web/client/operator/**`, `apps/web/components/ui/rail-nav.tsx`, `apps/web/config/operator-navigation.ts`
- **Reach**: On a configured operator test session, `goto "/admin"`, `expect "Overview"`; fixture-session alone cannot grant operator access.
- **Verify**: manual
- **Expect**: Overview has Needs attention then Business, each with an unavailable line; sidebar links to Customers, Support, Growth, Money, Settings and Audit log, each with a matching heading. Growth shows Invite links and Attributed sign-ups counts from the registry, or `Invite data isn't available.` when the read fails. Settings shows the regions editor and the Invest pane (Stocks, Crypto and Memes category switches and switches for each configured asset), each with built-in or saved source when persistence is available; without `DATABASE_URL` (fixture and smoke), `Region settings need a database. Home is offering its built-in regions.` and `Invest settings need a database. Home is showing its full catalog.`; when a read fails, `Region settings are unavailable. Try again shortly.` and `Invest settings are unavailable. Try again shortly.` An Invest row with no saved settings uses the built-in catalog without a write; saved rows show a local timestamp and operator address. Hidden discovery categories and assets do not block holdings or direct detail links. Settings also shows the Fees form (Swap fee in bps, Revenue destination, and a confirmation step when the destination changes) or `Fee settings couldn’t load`. Money shows Fee revenue (expected fees, a 30-day list, and recent fees with their result) or `Fee revenue is unavailable. Try again later.` The other sections retain unavailable lines. Current link has `aria-current="page"`; at 390px Sections opens the drawer, navigation closes it and restores menu focus. Signed-out requests redirect to sign-in; non-operator requests redirect to `/home`. The shell root carries `data-operator-shell` and gains `data-hydrated="true"` once React has hydrated it, so `tests/browser/admin.pw.ts` waits for `[data-operator-shell][data-hydrated="true"]` before its first click.
- **Evidence**: desktop and 390px screenshot, DOM snapshot, console/errors, keyboard and RTL navigation check.
- **Owned by**: `apps/web/app/admin/`, `apps/web/client/admin/`, `apps/web/client/operator/`, `apps/web/components/ui/rail-nav.tsx`, `apps/web/config/operator-navigation.ts`.
