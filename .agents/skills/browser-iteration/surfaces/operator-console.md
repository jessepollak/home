- **Entry context**: operator · `/admin`, `/admin/{customers,support,growth,money,settings,settings/products,audit}`, `/admin/settings/funding` · signed native Home operator session · fixture session signs the placeholder operator address for the shared fixture routes; operator allowlist and signed native session (browser admin.pw.ts) · `/admin/support` entry.
- **Live**: read-only (operator authorization required); take-over/reply/resolve/reopen/hand-back steps are fixture-only.
- **Owned paths**: `apps/web/app/admin/**`, `apps/web/client/admin/**`, `apps/web/client/operator/**`, `apps/web/client/operator-support/**`, `apps/web/components/ui/rail-nav.tsx`, `apps/web/config/operator-navigation.ts`
- **Reach**:
  1. `goto "/admin/support"`; the fixture session carries a signed operator session and the shared support-inbox fixture.
  2. `expect "fixture.customer@example.com"`.
  3. `goto "/admin/support/11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111"`.
  4. `expect "My funding order needs help"`.
  5. `click "Take over"`; `expect "An operator is handling this"`.
  6. `fill "Reply" "We can help"`; `click "Send"`; `expect "We can help"`.
  7. `click "Resolve"`; `expect "Reopen"`.
  8. `click "Reopen"`; `expect "Resolve"`.
  9. `click "Hand back to assistant"`; `expect "Assistant is handling this"`.

Changed-first-page and visibility refresh reset depth. Loading skeleton; error with `Try again`; Settings review drawer lists offered country changes and their effects; a conflict closes review, preserves the draft and updates the saved baseline; Invest category/asset toggles, a hidden category collapses its asset rows and keeps their individual choices, dirty draft/save pending/saved, 409 conflict adopts the latest value as both baseline and draft so the operator must re-apply their change, failed save allows retry; deployment and saved provenance, exit-only locked children, orphan IDs, 409 conflict/reload, enable review/cancel; `/admin/nope` and nested unknown paths are uncached 404s showing `Page not found` and `Back to Overview`, with no current link.
- **Money in and out** (`/admin/settings/funding`, linked from the Settings index; stories `journeys-admin-funding-settings--*`): deployment-values banner or last saved by/at; provider credential names as set/unset, never values; corridors by region as Connected, off / On / Not connected, with New for corridors added since the last save; `PEER_OFFRAMP_ENABLED` as in effect or set but ignored; notice for saved corridors no longer in the catalog. A not-connected corridor shows its switch on when its saved selection is on, labelled `Not connected · On when credentials are set`, and that switch can be turned off (pausing it) even though turning one on always needs credentials — a disconnected, off corridor's switch stays disabled, and losing a credential clears a pending activation with a notice. Turning any corridor on opens a review (credential status, the evidence note of every provider binding the switch covers, or `none recorded`, and what customers see) and applies only on Confirm; the review also groups corridors recorded on without credentials; pause-only saves confirm inline, naming disconnected corridors being paused and saying such a corridor stays off when its credentials return. States: saved, conflict (Save disabled until the latest settings load), uncertain save result, settings unavailable.
