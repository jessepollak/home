- **Layout**: below 1024px the header carries the Home mark and account mark, and Main navigation is one fixed, centred CSS glass capsule (192 × 60px, 52px tab targets) with Home/Invest, last scroll content clear above it. At ≥1024px a side rail (`aside#desktop-rail`, `Main navigation` with Home and Invest, a `Sidebar` toggle with `aria-expanded`, and the `Account settings` foot button `[data-rail-account-action]`) replaces both; the header keeps only the title, Back and status. Home is two columns there: money (with `data-sticky-fit` when it pins inside `main`) and Activity. Money dialogs open as a centred dialog at ≥1024px.
- **Expect**: `Total balance` card with `aria-label="Total balance"` (or `Balance unavailable` when the net is unavailable) and `aria-busy` while loading (home-overview.tsx) showing the net total from `totals.net`; signed allocation bar (`data-balance-breakdown`, `data-balance-segment="borrow|cash|pending-cash-out|investments"`, `data-balance-axis` when a known Borrow amount is present, components/signed-balance-bar.tsx) with its legend in Borrow, Cash, Pending cash-out, Investments order; a hatched Cash-green `Pending cash-out` item (`[data-pending-cash-out]`) appears only while an unsettled Peer cash-out still holds escrow, and its legend then uses two columns until the card is at least 28rem wide; tapping a segment or legend item toggles one selected category (available legend controls use `aria-pressed`, both segment and legend carry `data-selected`), without navigation or changing the total; a header status icon button `[data-home-status]` beside the account mark only when a balance read fails or no country is set (home-status.tsx; its accessible name is the message, and tapping it opens `[data-home-status-detail]`, named `Status`, with a `Retry` icon button, or `Open Account` when no country is set); a partial net keeps its foreground amount marked `[data-total-status]` with an independent tappable `Partial balance` control and no header status; the same header status also reports interrupted live updates on every verified dashboard screen (Home, Invest, nested panels) — `You’re offline. Home will update when you reconnect.` with no action after 2 foreground seconds offline, or `Home can’t refresh right now. Some information may be out of date.` with `Retry` after a failed balances confirmation read — and takes precedence over the Home statuses; money actions group `aria-label="Money actions"` with `Add money` and `Send`; `Your money` card (h2 `your-money-heading`) with exactly three rows — Cash (opens `/cash`), Investments (opens `/investments` when holdings exist, or `/invest` for the empty `Start investing` row), Borrow Cash (opens `/borrow`); Activity section card (same card treatment as Your money, h2 `activity-title`, `data-activity-feed` on the section) with a centered spinner (`data-activity-loader`) while older rows load, a centered 44 px `Add money` prompt (`data-activity-nux`) when it is empty, and a centered muted `[data-activity-unavailable]` line with a `Reload activity` icon button when an Activity read fails. The default fixture has no Borrow position, so Borrow Cash renders its no-position context.
  Partial total (`[data-total-status="partial"]`) keeps the exact known amount still and foreground. `Total balance` stays visible (`#home-total-label`); a quiet tappable `Partial balance` control (`#home-total-status`) opens source-specific details and `Retry balances` when supplied. The figure references both labels with `aria-describedby`. Affected summary rows show a brief reason or `Partial balance` for multiple limitations; complete rows have no status chrome. Amounts in all summary rows and legend entries stay foreground; refresh failure remains independent. An unavailable total shows `—` (accessible as `Unavailable`), renames the card `Balance unavailable`, and shows `Retry total balance` beside it when recovery exists and no country choice is needed. The legend keeps an unavailable component as a disabled `—` entry (`data-breakdown-status="unavailable"`, `aria-disabled`, out of the tab order, no `aria-pressed`, on the same control node so an entry changing status keeps keyboard focus); it draws no bar segment, and the Borrow axis and segment appear only while a known Borrow amount exists — so they are absent for an unavailable Borrow figure and for no Borrow entry at all. Partial entries show the known amount without animation. An entry that disappears, or a whole breakdown that empties, removes its controls and leaves focus on the document.
  Partial details belong only to active Home: navigation or browser history that makes the retained Home panel inactive unmounts the popup and its Retry action. Returning to Home starts with the explanation closed; no overlay or focus remains in the hidden/inert Home subtree.
- **Pull-to-refresh**: Only the verified Home scroll owner captures a downward drag from the top; the header and navigation stay pinned while the content moves. A keyboard-focusable, icon-only action named `Refresh Home` replaces the visible indicator circle on focus and invokes the same refresh, with polite `Refreshing Home` / `Home updated` announcements. A settled failed or partial read keeps the Home content visible and shows `Couldn't refresh Home.` or `Some of Home didn't refresh.` with `Retry` at the top of the content. The gesture is disabled while a money flow or sign-in sheet is open, and both the action and gesture are absent on other panels and in Account settings; check that the scroll position is not forced back during refresh.
- **States** (fixtures): loading → hold `/api/session`/`/api/balances` with `fixtures.delayNextSession()/delayNextBalances()` (balances.pw.ts and cash.pw.ts); empty → base fixture minus holdings (**no ready empty fixture exists — construct via `options.balances`**); unavailable → `status: "unavailable"` presentation (home-overview.tsx `Balance unavailable` hero, foreground `—` rows without chevrons, header status with `Retry`); offline → `set offline on` for at least 2 s after the dashboard is signed in; interrupted → a balances route returning 5xx on the refresh and on the 5 s confirmation read; refresh gesture states → idle, pulling, armed, refreshing, settling, partial failure and failed with Retry (journey story); action-submission errors are surfaced in the modal, not the panel.
